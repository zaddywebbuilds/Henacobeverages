Before you message

The questions everybody asks

How much is a carton?
Prices move with the market, so we do not print them on the site — you would only be reading an old number. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send you the current list for whichever brands you are interested in.
What is the smallest order you accept?
Tell us what you need and we will work with you. Whether you are testing one carton for your shop or loading a truck for your area, message us and we will confirm the minimum for your order and your location.
Can you deliver to my state?
Yes — we deliver anywhere in Nigeria from our factory in Aba, Abia State. Send us your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm the delivery arrangement and cost for that route.
Are your products NAFDAC registered?
Yes. Our products carry NAFDAC registration numbers printed on the labels — for example Heatman's Aromatic Schiedam Schnapps (01-6160), Makossa Coconut Liqueur (01-3448), Lord Henaco Brandy (01-5341) and Commissionale Fine Brandy (A1-5139).
Can I mix different brands in one order?
That is exactly what we recommend for a first order. Take a few cartons across brandy, rum, schnapps and liqueur, see what your customers reach for, then load up on the winners next time.
How do I pay?
We will confirm the accepted payment arrangement with you on WhatsApp before your order is packed. Always confirm details directly with us on +234 806 808 2495 — do not send money to any account that did not come from that number.
Do you also supply table water?
Yes — Henaco bottles water as well as spirits. Message us about water supply for your shop, your event or a standing weekly delivery and we will quote you.
